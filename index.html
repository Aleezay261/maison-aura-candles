The Chandler’s Wisdom

The Candle Care Ritual

Caring for a botanical candle extends its life and ensures an uninterrupted, soot-free fragrant diffusion. Follow our simple rituals for an optimal experience.

Order With Complimentary Gift Box

01. The First Burn Memory

Allow the wax pool to reach the outer glass edge upon first lighting (approx. 2 to 3 hours). Wax holds memory and this prevents tunneling.

02. Trim the Cotton Wick

Before every relighting, gently trim the wick to 5mm (1/4 inch). A trimmed wick ensures a steady flame, zero soot, and extends burn duration by 20%.

03. Peaceful Extinction

Extinguish your candle using a bell snuffer or by gently dipping the wick into the molten wax pool, eliminating after-smoke and protecting the aroma.
