The Olfactory Pyramid

Understanding Candle Scent Structure

Just like bespoke fine perfume, each Maison Aura candle is constructed across three evolving olfactory stages.

First 15 Minutes

Top Notes (Tête)

The first impression experienced immediately upon striking the match. Volatile, refreshing notes such as Italian bergamot, neroli petals, cardamom, and fresh fig leaf.

After 30 Minutes

Heart Notes (Cœur)

The authentic personality of the candle that emerges once the wax pool warms. Rich botanical essences like Damask rose, Australian sandalwood, coconut milk, and cypress.

Sustained Aura

Base Notes (Fond)

The heaviest molecules that linger in your home hours after the flame has rested. Golden amber resin, bourbon vanilla pod, Cambodian oud, and white cedarwood.
